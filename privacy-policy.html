Shelf Chrome Extension

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

🔒

Short version: Shelf stores the page or link you choose to save, its title, your optional note, and its reminder settings in Chrome's local extension storage. Shelf does not send this information to the developer or to external servers.

What Shelf Stores

Shelf accesses a page's URL and title only when you invoke the extension or choose a Shelf command from the context menu. It does not read pages or monitor browsing in the background. When you save a page or link, Shelf stores the following information in Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • The URL of the saved page
  • The page title (editable by you)
  • An optional note you write yourself
  • The date and time you want to be reminded
  • Whether the reminder repeats daily

This information stays in your Chrome profile on your device. Shelf does not transmit it, sell it, or share it with third parties. The developer does not receive it.

What Shelf Does NOT Do

  • Does not connect to any external server or API
  • Does not collect analytics, usage data, or crash reports
  • Does not read pages or monitor browsing activity in the background
  • Does not use cookies
  • Does not share any information with third parties
  • Does not sell any data — ever
  • Does not require account creation or sign-in

Permissions Explained

  • storage — saves your shelf items locally on your device
  • alarms — schedules reminder notifications at your chosen time
  • notifications — displays reminder and confirmation pop-ups
  • contextMenus — adds options to save a page or link from the right-click menu
  • activeTab — reads the current page's URL and title after you invoke Shelf, so it can save the page you chose

Data Deletion

You can delete any saved item from the extension popup. Uninstalling Shelf removes its locally stored extension data from your Chrome profile.

Because no data is ever sent externally, there is nothing to request deletion of from any server.

Children's Privacy

Shelf is not designed to collect information from children. A URL or note that you choose to save may contain information you consider personal; Shelf keeps it in Chrome's local extension storage and does not send it to the developer.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the “Last updated” date above will reflect the change. Any significant changes will be noted in the Chrome Web Store listing update notes.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Open an issue on the Shelf GitHub repository or reach out directly.
