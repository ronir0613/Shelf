Shelf Chrome Extension

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025  ·  Effective immediately

🔒

Short version: Shelf stores everything you save — page URLs, titles, notes, and reminder times — exclusively on your own device. No data is ever sent to external servers, collected by the developer, or shared with any third party.

What Shelf Stores

When you save a page to Shelf, the following information is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API (chrome.storage.local):

  • The URL of the saved page
  • The page title (editable by you)
  • An optional note you write yourself
  • The date and time you want to be reminded
  • Whether the reminder repeats daily

This data is stored only on your local device within your Chrome browser profile. It is never transmitted anywhere.

What Shelf Does NOT Do

  • Does not connect to any external server or API
  • Does not collect analytics, usage data, or crash reports
  • Does not track your browsing history or reading habits
  • Does not use cookies
  • Does not share any information with third parties
  • Does not sell any data — ever
  • Does not require account creation or sign-in

Permissions Explained

  • storage — saves your shelf items locally on your device
  • alarms — schedules reminder notifications at your chosen time
  • notifications — displays reminder and confirmation pop-ups
  • contextMenus — adds “Shelf This Page” to the right-click menu
  • activeTab — reads the URL and title of the current tab when you click the icon
  • tabs — opens a saved page in a new tab when a reminder fires

Data Deletion

You can delete any saved item at any time directly from the extension popup. Uninstalling Shelf from Chrome will permanently remove all locally stored data.

Because no data is ever sent externally, there is nothing to request deletion of from any server.

Children's Privacy

Shelf does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected or transmitted at all, there are no special provisions required.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the “Last updated” date above will reflect the change. Any significant changes will be noted in the Chrome Web Store listing update notes.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Open an issue on the Shelf GitHub repository or reach out directly.
